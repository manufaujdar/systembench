# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not
share private evaluation sets, production prompts or traces, personal data,
credentials, proprietary model artifacts, or confidential system details in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or patches.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, benchmark
gaming, and unsupported claims about safety or human capability are not
acceptable. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to protect contributors, evaluation subjects, users, or the project.

Use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for sensitive
security, privacy, or benchmark-integrity concerns.
